Frequently Asked Questions

How does the onboarding process work? +
Our team of security engineers will work directly with you to integrate Aegis into your existing stack. Typical onboarding takes less than 48 hours with zero downtime for your services.
Can I upgrade my plan later? +
Absolutely. You can scale up to the Enterprise tier at any time from your dashboard. Your billing will be pro-rated for the remainder of the month.
What counts as an "endpoint"? +
An endpoint is any physical or virtual device that connects directly to your secure network perimeter, including employee laptops, cloud servers, and IoT devices.
